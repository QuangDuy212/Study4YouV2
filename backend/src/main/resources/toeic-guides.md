# TOEIC Exam Structure & Tips

## Part 1: Photographs
- **Structure**: 6 questions. 
- **Tips**: Focus on main subjects, verbs, and prepositions. Watch for distractor statements.

## Part 2: Question-Response
- **Structure**: 25 questions. 
- **Tips**: Identify question words (Who, Why, etc.). Avoid repeating-word traps.

## Part 3 & 4 (Conversations & Talks)
- **Structure**: Multi-speaker conversations or single speaker talks.
- **Tips**: Read questions first. Identify speakers/setting.

## Part 5: Incomplete Sentences
- **Structure**: 30 grammar/vocab questions.
- **Grammar focus**: Subject-verb agreement, tenses, parts of speech, prepositions.

## Part 6: Text Completion
- **Tips**: Use whole-paragraph context.

## Part 7: Reading Comprehension
- **Structure**: Single/double/triple passages.
- **Tips**: Scan for keywords. Focus on proper names/dates.
